Privacy Policy for Pipe Connect

Effective Date: November 8, 2026  |  Package Name: com.pipeconnect.game  |  Developer: PipeConnect Studio

PipeConnect Studio ("we," "our," or "us") built the Pipe Connect mobile application (the "App") as a free-to-play puzzle game. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal and device data when you use our App, in compliance with Google Play's User Data Policy.

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

We collect and process the following categories of data to operate and improve the App:

  • Gameplay Progress & Local Storage: Your level progression, star ratings, high scores, audio settings, and 8-hour challenge timers are stored locally on your device using Unity PlayerPrefs.
  • Optional Account & Leaderboard Data: If you choose to create an in-game profile or submit scores to the global leaderboard, we process your chosen display name (or guest identifier), optional email address, and level/challenge scores solely to display leaderboard standings and synchronize your profile.
  • Advertising Data (Google AdMob): The App uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded video advertisements. Google AdMob may automatically collect and process your device's Advertising ID (AAID), coarse location (country/region derived from IP address), device model, operating system version, and ad interaction events (views and clicks) for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and analytics.
  • Diagnostics & Crash Logs: Anonymous crash reports and performance diagnostics may be collected via Google Play Services to identify and fix bugs and stability issues.

2. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. The App integrates third-party SDKs that may collect data as governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob & AdSense Privacy Policy

3. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local Game Data: You can clear all locally saved game progress and profile data at any time by clearing the App's storage in your Android device settings or uninstalling the App.
  • Account / Leaderboard Data Deletion: You may log out or switch to a Guest profile inside the App's Account & Profile panel at any time. To request permanent deletion of any leaderboard or account record associated with your identifier, contact us at the email address below and we will process your deletion request within 7 business days.
  • Advertising ID Reset / Opt-Out: You can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your Android device under Settings → Google → Ads.

4. Security

We value your trust in providing your data and use commercially acceptable security measures, including encrypted HTTPS/TLS transmission for network requests, to protect user information.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can promptly delete such information.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or data deletion requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact PipeConnect Studio via the developer contact email listed on the Pipe Connect Google Play Store page.
